# Course illustration catalogue

All 15 lessons include one schematic concept illustration in `course.html`. Open a lesson and scroll to the section listed below. Images are inserted after the section text, unless a before-heading boundary is specified. Keep the entire `assets/` directory beside the HTML when opening or sharing it.

The series uses restrained textbook ink on warm paper, one idea per image, sparse labels, and generous empty space. The original image colors remain stable in both themes; captions and frames follow the theme. On mobile, images scale without cropping and have a full-size link beneath them. English/Persian captions, alt text, and translated label explanations come from the manifest. These concept illustrations do not replace numerical plots of measured study results.

Generation mode: built-in `image_gen`. Prompt sets: [Lessons 1–5](preview_set_01/prompts.json), [Lessons 6–9](prompts_lessons_06_09.json), [Lessons 10–15](prompts_lessons_10_15.json), and [Lesson 13 revision](lesson_13_revision_prompt.json). The first approved image is the style reference. The revised Lesson 13 image is the active asset; its first draft is retained for production history.
